Install and play.

Extract the ZIP into a writable folder, open the launcher and press Play. Release builds offer public updates. Testing builds stay on the bundled version; install each new testing build in a fresh folder and copy saves/ to keep your progress.

If Linux executable permissions are missing after extraction, run chmod +x Molderia-Launcher engine/RuneGame.

Your first spell starts here.

Choose New to begin with 10 HP, 2 mana, and three runes: d2, Damage, and Fire. The Kenning lives in your Portable Hole. A suspicious certificate on a pedestal grants full demo power for testing.

  • Arrow keys or controller stick: walk.
  • Z / Enter / A: talk, inspect, confirm.
  • Tab / Start: equip runes. I / Back: open the bag.
  • Space: timing beats. 1–8: rune hotkeys.

Your folder.

The launcher installs the game into engine/ and the base game's mods into system_mods/, replacing them on each update. Your journeys live in saves/, and mods you add go in user_mods/; updates never touch either. Mods signed by the developer show a green check on the Mods screen.

Windows may show an unknown-publisher warning for the launcher. Linux needs a graphical desktop with the usual OpenGL, audio and keyboard (libxkbcommon) libraries.
