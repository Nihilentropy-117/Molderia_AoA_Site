Your first spell starts here.

Choose New to begin with 10 HP, 2 mana, and three runes: d2, Damage, and Fire. The Kenning lives in your Portable Hole. A suspicious certificate on a pedestal grants full demo power for testing.

  • Arrow keys or controller stick: walk.
  • Z / Enter / A: talk, inspect, confirm.
  • Tab / Start: equip runes. I / Back: open the bag.
  • Space: timing beats. 1–8: rune hotkeys.

Keep your journey.

The game writes player.toml beside the executable. Keep that file when replacing the game with a new build. Put the game in a folder you can write to.

This is an unsigned development demo. Windows may display an unknown-publisher warning. Linux needs a working graphical desktop and the usual OpenGL/audio libraries.
